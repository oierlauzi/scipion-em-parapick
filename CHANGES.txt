0.1.0 (unreleased):
    - First version.
    - Protocol "unstack tilt-series": SetOfTiltSeries -> SetOfMicrographs (one micrograph per tilt image) for SPA pickers.
      The output is a plain SetOfMicrographs of Micrograph items, which Xmipp's picker and viewer require; the tilt
      identity is kept in extra attributes. Outputs of the former SetOfTiltSeriesMicrographs class still load.
    - Viewer "analyze results" for localize: per-tilt sigma, residuals and detection vs tilt angle (box/interval), scores, positions, per tilt-series summary.
    - Protocol "localize": 2D picks -> SetOfTiltSeriesCoordinates, SetOfLandmarkModels (pick chains) and, optionally, SetOfCoordinates3D.
