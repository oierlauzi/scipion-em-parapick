0.1.0 (unreleased):
    - First version.
    - Protocol "template matching (Leopard-EM)": SetOfTiltSeries + SetOfCTFTomoSeries + template -> SetOfMicrographs
      (the tilt images) and SetOfCoordinates scored by z-score, ready for "localize". Runs "parapick match-template",
      with the parameters of each tilt derived from its dose and geometry. Needs the optional binary parapick-leopard.
      Leopard-EM (Giammar et al., 2026, Acta Cryst. D) is cited in the protocol's references and methods.
      Options "Binning factor" and "Sampling" (default/fast) for faster searches.
    - Protocol "unstack tilt-series": SetOfTiltSeries -> SetOfMicrographs (one micrograph per tilt image) for SPA pickers.
      The output is a plain SetOfMicrographs of Micrograph items, which Xmipp's picker and viewer require; the tilt
      identity is kept in extra attributes. Outputs of the former SetOfTiltSeriesMicrographs class still load.
      Optionally takes a SetOfCTFTomoSeries and also outputs the CTF of each micrograph as a SetOfCTF.
    - Viewer "analyze results" for localize: per-tilt sigma, residuals and detection vs tilt angle (box/interval), scores, positions, per tilt-series summary.
    - Protocol "localize": 2D picks -> SetOfTiltSeriesCoordinates, SetOfLandmarkModels (pick chains) and, optionally, SetOfCoordinates3D.
